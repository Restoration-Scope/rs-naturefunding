# Finder and listing pages (naturefunding.org)

Scope: the finder (home), programme pages /p/<id>/, and /about/. Visitor mode: Operate on the finder; Read on programme and about pages.

Audience and job: restoration NGO staff and project developers looking for money they can apply for; they filter by status, place, approach, applicant type and access route, and need to trust the deadline. Proof: every fact's source quote and checked date. Constraints: static, no server, no accounts, WCAG AA, phone width, light and dark.

Chosen direction: "Plot kit", the field crew's marking kit (stamped aluminium tree tags and fluorescent flagging tape), held to type, palette, density and one signature move. Chosen unattended: Bernat away; no decision page was served. Grounded list (by resonance): herbarium sheet label, topographic survey map, waterproof field notebook, IUCN Red List scale, trail blazes, tree tags and flagging tape (assigned, 6), seed packets.

Challenger verdicts: split-flap board competitive (clarity), kept its fixed column rhythm. Transit diagram competitive (clarity), kept one constant mark per access route. Botanical folio declined, kept identical card anatomy on shared baselines. Metro tiles declined, kept chrome deleted so type carries the interface. Star atlas and vertical feed declined.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A listing is only as good as its provenance. Every fact wears its tag: where it was read and when. Refuses the category default of a grey card grid with a green "Open" pill and no date.

OWN-WORLD: Cool aluminium-grey ground (not cream), spruce-black ink, a deep spruce shell band. Flagging-tape inks as state: tape green for open, tape blue for rolling, tape orange for upcoming, grey for closed, amber for a stale check. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all text; Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for stamped data (dates, amounts, IDs). Hairline rules, 4px corners, no shadows except the raised filter panel on phones.

STORY: The visitor reads one honest sentence, filters to what they can apply for, sees the deadline in the funder's words and a stamped "Checked" tag, opens the programme page and reads each fact with its quote and source link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Spruce band with name, one-sentence purpose and the count of open calls. Below, left a filter column (search, status, deadline, where, approach, applicant, access, size); right the result list in a fixed column rhythm: status as a short flagging-tape label (tape colour, word inside, notched end), programme and funder, deadline as written, amount, access route, checked tag at right. Phone: filters collapse into one "Filters" button over the list.

FORM: tree tags and flagging tape, 6 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:a67d063c1b5d9ce0. Signature move: the stamped aluminium "Checked" tag with punched hole on every listing and every fact, going amber after 30 days.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
