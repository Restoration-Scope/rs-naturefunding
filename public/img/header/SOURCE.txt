The home page header image.

Source: naturefunding-header-mj.jpeg, a 1536×768 image Bernat Fortet made with Midjourney for naturefunding.org
(a misty forest, two people planting seedlings). The original is not in the repository.

Made with scripts/images/header-image.ts (sharp):
  bun scripts/images/header-image.ts path/to/naturefunding-header-mj.jpeg

wide-*: the clearing right of the dark trees, beside the text on tablets and wider (640 and 1096 px wide).
strip-*: the planters and the seedling row, under the text on phones (480 and 768 px wide).
Each as AVIF, WebP and JPEG.
