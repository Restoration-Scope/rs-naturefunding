## Decision

Grants that pay an operator to design, scope or permit a specific restoration project (Chesapeake Bay Trust Watershed Assistance, Riverwoods Development Grant) are listed. Pure planning or strategy grants with no project behind them stay out.

## Why

Design is the first stage of field work, and operators look for it as money for their project. The catch: these grants don't pay for the work on the ground itself, and one writer rejected the CBT grant for that reason.
