## Decision

Ecosia and Arbor Day Foundation are listed even though they pay planting organisations under partnership contracts rather than award grants. Their summaries say so.

## Why

For a restoration NGO they are money for planting, which is what visitors look for. The catch: they're not grants, and Arbor Day's page only offers an interest form.
