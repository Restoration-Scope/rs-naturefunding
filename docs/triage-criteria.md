# Triage criteria

Use this checklist to decide whether an automated scanner should accept, hold, or reject a funding opportunity for naturefunding.org. Some scanner error rate is acceptable; when evidence is missing or ambiguous, hold the opportunity for a human rather than inferring.

## Accept checklist

Accept only when every item is true:

- **Operator-led nature work.** The applicant can lead the project on the ground as an organisation or individual field-project lead.
- **Restoration or conservation grant.** The funded work protects, restores, conserves or improves habitats, species, ecosystems or natural resources. Equipment grants can fit when the equipment supports field projects.
- **First-party funder source.** The call is described on the funder's own page, or an official page from the funder-appointed administrator.
- **Verbatim evidence.** Eligibility, amount, deadline and region each have a first-party `source_url`, verbatim `quote` and `checked_at`.
- **Deadlines are stated.** A deadline is recorded only when the first-party page states it, including the year needed for the date.

## Hold checklist

Hold for human review when any item is true:

- The opportunity looks eligible, but only a committee paper, PDF agenda, aggregator, directory or news mirror has the live details.
- A deadline, amount, geography or eligibility rule appears only on an aggregator or third-party guide.
- A first-party page exists, but the scanner cannot fetch or quote it.
- A mixed-scope programme has eligible nature areas and ineligible areas, and the entry needs a note to separate them.
- The amount, applicant type or project-lead role is unclear.

## Reject checklist

Reject when any item is true:

- Farmer or landowner payment scheme rather than a grant for project operators.
- Grant only for students or academic research.
- Research-only call without on-the-ground restoration or conservation delivery.
- Open only to public agencies.
- Heritage, education, planning, strategy, advice, contracts or membership benefits without direct field-project grant funding.
- Closed call where a new deadline is not first-party verified.
- No stated amount when the programme is optional, incidental or otherwise too weak to list.

## Worked examples

- **ACCEPT NatureScot Nature Restoration Fund** - habitat, freshwater, coastal and urban restoration; charities and community groups eligible.
- **ACCEPT Minnesota DNR ENRTF Community Impact round** - nonprofits and Tribes can apply; natural resources and aquatic invasive species areas fit, with trail and education areas noted out of scope.
- **HOLD South Cambridgeshire Nature Recovery Grant** - only in a council committee PDF, not yet on the council's live grants page; wait for a live funder page.
- **HOLD CAF RE-SET 2026** - the deadline comes only from aggregators; needs a first-party capture.
- **REJECT DAERA Farming with Nature** - farmer-payment scheme.
- **REJECT World Monuments Fund grants** - heritage, not nature.
- **REJECT Jackson SWCD Oregon** - mostly education and farming.
- **REJECT NABU BW Gemeinschaftsgrün** - in-kind advice plus €500 for materials; too small and not a grant.
- **REJECT SOS2LearnDBS** - funds strategic roadmaps, not on-the-ground work.
- **REJECT DFO AERF** - closed; the 2026 deadline came only from a third-party site.
- **REJECT Oilwatch** - members only.
- **REJECT Trees for Tribs** - coordinator contract, not a grant.
- **REJECT Bayerisches Naturerbe** - optional and no amount stated.
